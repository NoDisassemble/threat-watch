Attribution caveat

A catalog, not a live activity ranking

Threat Watch includes MISP Galaxy entries with structured country or suspected-state-sponsor metadata. Attribution is contested and may describe a threat actor, activity cluster, campaign, or operation. Inclusion does not prove government control, current activity, or relative danger.

About the feed

Observed community activity

DShield aggregates firewall, Cowrie SSH/Telnet, and web honeypot reports. These are observational signals, not proof that an address is currently malicious. Threat Watch retrieves the public feeds server-side, removes malformed display values, and follows DShield's one-hour cache guidance.

How it works

Calculating the ransomware top ten

Threat Watch deduplicates RansomLook posts by group and victim title, limits the data to the latest 30 calendar days, then ranks groups by claim volume with seven-day activity as a tie-breaker. Claims are unverified leak-site assertions—not confirmed intrusions, payments, or a complete measure of ransomware impact.
